Retail Sales Dashboard – Tableau Project

This project presents a simple, clean "Retail Sales Dashboard" built using "Tableau", designed to provide insights into sales performance based on product category, region, and time.

Project Files:
- "dashboard.png" – Screenshot of the completed Tableau dashboard
- "Insights.txt" – 4 summarized business insights derived from the dashboard

 Features of the Dashboard:
- Line Chart: Sales over time (segmented by customer type: Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- Bar Chart: Top products by sales
- Map: Sales distribution by region (U.S. states)
- Pie Chart: Sales breakdown by product category
- Scatter Plot: Profit vs. Discount (product-level analysis)
- Bar Chart: Yearly sales and year-over-year growth comparison

Business Insights:
The following insights were drawn directly from the visualizations:
1. California generated the highest overall sales in the United States.  
2. Sales peaked in November, with the Corporate segment showing strong performance.  
3. Copiers and Accessories contributed the most profit despite various discount levels.  
4. Year-over-year sales grew the most in 2016, showing a 29.47% increase.

Tools Used:
- Tableau Desktop
- Dataset: Superstore Sales (CSV format)

Outcome:
This project demonstrates basic data visualization capabilities in Tableau, useful for entry-level business analysts or students learning BI tools.
